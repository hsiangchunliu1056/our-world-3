Unit 3 — On the Move! Sentence Patterns
Grammar 1
1. A: I ride my scooter to school. B: I do, too.
2. A: I take the bus to school. B: I don't. I take the subway.
3. A: My brother rides his bike to school. B: My brother does, too.
4. A: My sister rides her skateboard to school. B: My sister doesn't. She walks.
Grammar 2
5. My mother takes a bus to work, but my father takes the subway.
6. The boy rides his scooter to school, but the girl rides her bike to school.
7. The girl eats breakfast at eight o'clock, but the boy eats breakfast at seven thirty.
8. The boy has a pet rabbit, but the girl has a pet dog.
9. He wants to be a singer, but she wants to be a vet.
10. The girl likes spaghetti for lunch, but the boy likes hamburgers for lunch.
11. Jenny likes to play tennis on Saturdays, but Sam likes to play soccer on Saturdays.
